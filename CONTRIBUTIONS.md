# Contributions

A ledger of who contributed what to the proof, its checking and its explanation. Each entry links to a
public record. Entries describe what the record shows; they are not endorsements or verdicts.
Missing or misattributed contributions: please open an issue.

## Format

Each entry: **who** (name or handle) — **what** (one sentence) — **kind** — **status** — **record** (link, date).

Kinds: `proof` · `formalization` · `compilation/kernel check` · `review` · `exposition` ·
`errata/gap` · `fix` · `commentary` · `this book`.
